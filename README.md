# SimpleRevive

Revive your teammates without going back to the truck.

## How to use
1. Pick up the head of a dead player.
2. Hold **H** for 2 seconds. A green bar under the crosshair fills up, with a rising tone; let go early to cancel.
3. Reviving costs **10% of your health, at least 10**. The revived player gets exactly the
   health you paid:

| Your health | You | Revived player |
|---|---|---|
| 150 | lose 15 → 135 | 15 health |
| 100 | lose 10 → 90 | 10 health |
| 40 | lose 10 → 30 | 10 health |
| 10 | die | 10 health |
| 6 | die | 6 health |

## Config
- `SoundVolume` (0 to 1, default 0.6): volume of the revive sounds. Set it to 0 to turn them off.

## Requirements
- **The host** and **anyone who wants to revive** need the mod.
  The player being revived doesn't need it.
